How racing works
1

Two timed heats, everyone races both

A qualifier, then a final — no disqualifications, so nobody's out early. Expect breaks in between, not back-to-back racing.

2

Budget about 1–1.5 hours

Racing plus breaks between heats, not back-to-back.

3

Gear you keep

Helmet's provided to race in. You take home your headsock, and medals go to the top 3 finishers.

4

Party room + arcade after

We've got the party room for an hour once racing wraps, and arcade play cards are provided — no tokens needed.

Before you race
✓

Closed-toe shoes, mandatory

Sandals and open-toe shoes aren't allowed on the track — you won't be able to race without them.

✓

Height check for karts

58" and taller races an adult kart; 48"–58" (age 8+) races a junior kart. Adult and junior racers don't share a heat, so shorter siblings tagging along will race separately.

✓

Skip loose clothing

Scarves, long dresses, or anything flowy can snag inside the kart. Ponytail your hair if it's long — helmets are provided.

✓

Waiver, before you arrive

Every racer needs a signed liability waiver, and K1 doesn't offer paper ones. Register online ahead of time (link below) — it signs you straight into our reservation, so check-in is quick instead of waiting at a kiosk.

Heads up: anyone under 18 needs a parent or legal guardian to sign their waiver — it can't be signed by another adult on their behalf.
